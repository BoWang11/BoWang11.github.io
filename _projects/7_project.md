**Data Source**: [Credit Risk Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/laotse/credit-risk-dataset)

**GitHub Link**: [Credit Risk Decision Engine MyByz Version 1.0](https://github.com/BoWang11/MyByz1.0)

**Key Skills**: Classification (e.g., Random Forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM classifiers), Python, Git, GCP, Flask, SHAP

**Summary**: This project demonstrated the power of machine learning in financial risk management, showcasing its ability to outperform traditional methods in accuracy, efficiency, and scalability. Here is a project summary following the STAR method:

- Situation:
  - Financial institutions faced challenges in accurately evaluating loan risks due to traditional credit assessment methods being time-consuming, labor-intensive, and prone to uncertainty. This inefficiency hindered business growth and increased financial risks.

- Task:
  - The goal was to develop a machine learning-based solution to predict loan defaults more accurately and create a loan decision engine to enable real-time credit risk evaluation, reducing human bias and improving efficiency.

- Action:
  - Built and optimized machine learning models (Random Forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM) to predict loan defaults, achieving precision and recall scores above 0.75 despite working with imbalanced datasets.
  - Enhanced model performance through feature engineering and hyperparameter tuning.
  - Deployed a loan decision engine as an API via Flask on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) to enable automated, near-real-time loan decisions.
  - Integrated SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to enhance model interpretability, allowing financial institutions to identify key drivers of loan defaults and implement targeted risk mitigation strategies.

- Result:
  - The new credit risk assessment system reduced loan decision times by 50%.
  - The predictive model helped financial institutions save over $1 million in default losses.
  - Improved efficiency and accuracy in credit risk evaluation, minimizing human error and enhancing decision-making processes.
